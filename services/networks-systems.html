Questions

Questions & answers

Can you set up and manage our systems remotely?
Most of the work (planning, configuration, cloud services, accounts and security) is done remotely. When hardware has to be installed on site, we guide you or work with a local technician.
Do you work with Microsoft 365?
Yes: setup, mailboxes and domains, file sharing, user accounts and security settings.
Can you look after our systems over time?
Yes. After the setup, we can keep systems updated and monitored, and handle changes as your team grows. The details are agreed in your written quote.
Will we get documentation?
Yes. You get documentation of how everything is set up, and the accounts stay in your name.
How do quotes work?
We start with a short conversation about your setup and what is not working. You then get a written quote with the scope, timeline and price before any work starts.
